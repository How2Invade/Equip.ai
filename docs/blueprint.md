# **App Name**: EquipAI

## Core Features:

- Profile Analyzer: Analyze user profiles from LinkedIn, Github or smart questionnaire to extract and map skills.
- Career Roadmap Generator: Generate personalized career roadmaps based on user skills and real-time market data.
- Skills Lag Score: Provide an analysis of how a user's skills compare to the industry average with breakdown by category. Suggest specific improvements.
- AI Career Counselor: An AI career counselor, acting as a tool, will provide real-time career guidance.
- Interactive Roadmap Visualization: Display career roadmaps with clickable skill nodes and milestone achievements.
- Progress Tracking: Monitor progress and track skills unlocked in the learning hub.
- AI Chat Interface: Provide a real-time chat interface for users to interact with the AI counselor.
- Voice Career Consultation: Users record career aspirations and concerns for AI analysis. Podcast recommendations and voice-based skill assessments.
- Peer Comparison Engine: Compare progress against similar users without privacy concerns. Access to career journeys of professionals who achieved similar goals.
- Learning ROI Calculator: Expected income increase from acquiring specific skills. Cost-benefit analysis of different learning paths.
- Industry Trend Predictor: 2-year projections for emerging and declining competencies. Notifications about trending job openings and career shifts.
- Portfolio Generator: Dynamic resume creation based on skills and achievements. GitHub, Behance, and LinkedIn portfolio compilation.
- Company Culture Matching: Assessment of ideal workplace culture and values. Matching with organizations that align with user values.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Dark vibrant blue (#133E87) to convey trust and competence.
- Background color: Very light blue (#CBDCEB) to provide a clean, professional backdrop.
- Accent color: Light blue (#608BC1) for CTAs and highlights, providing an energetic contrast.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' sans-serif, providing a modern, machined and objective look. Suitable for headlines or body text.
- Use modern, minimalist icons to represent different career paths and skills.
- A clean, data-focused layout will emphasize the importance of informed decision making.
- Subtle transitions and micro-interactions will enhance the user experience without being distracting.
